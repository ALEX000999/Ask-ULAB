# UNIVERSITY INFO
ULAB_GENERAL_001: University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB) is a private liberal arts university located in Dhanmondi, Dhaka, Bangladesh.
ULAB_GENERAL_002: ULAB was founded in February 2002 and received permission from the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh in November 2003.
ULAB_GENERAL_003: ULAB incorporates the values of Liberal Arts and Sustainability into its curricula through its General Education Department and Center for Sustainable Development.
ULAB_GENERAL_004: ULAB is a research-intensive university with multiple research opportunities for faculty members and students.
ULAB_GENERAL_005: ULAB has achieved a milestone in Times Higher Education Sustainability Impact Rankings 2026.

# ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
ULAB_ADMISSION_001: ULAB undergraduate admission requires minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC and HSC examinations.
ULAB_ADMISSION_002: Students with at least one GPA of 2.00 but aggregate GPA of 6.00 in SSC and HSC are eligible for ULAB admission.
ULAB_ADMISSION_003: Sons and daughters of freedom fighters need an aggregate GPA of 5.00 in SSC and HSC for ULAB admission.
ULAB_ADMISSION_004: O Level students need minimum GPA 2.50 in 5 subjects and A Level students need GPA 2.00 in 2 subjects for ULAB admission.
ULAB_ADMISSION_005: International Baccalaureate and American High School Diploma holders are eligible for ULAB admission.
ULAB_ADMISSION_006: Admission test is waived for candidates with minimum SAT score of 1100 in Math and Critical Reading at ULAB.
ULAB_ADMISSION_007: ULAB admission test Part 1 covers English, Mathematics, Physics and Logical Reasoning as MCQ.
ULAB_ADMISSION_008: ULAB admission test Part 2 is a written English essay test.
ULAB_ADMISSION_009: ULAB calls applicants who meet minimum eligibility for an admission test and sometimes a departmental interview.
ULAB_ADMISSION_010: Documents needed for ULAB admission: two passport-sized photos, board mark-sheets, certificates, NID photocopy.
ULAB_ADMISSION_011: Incomplete applications are not accepted at ULAB. False information leads to immediate disqualification.
ULAB_ADMISSION_012: ULAB offers admission in three sessions every year: Spring, Summer and Fall.
ULAB_ADMISSION_013: ULAB application is available online at admissions.ulab.edu.bd and also at 688 Beribadh Road, Mohammadpur office.

# SCHOLARSHIPS
ULAB_SCHOLAR_001: ULAB gives 100% tuition scholarship to students with GPA 5.0 in both SSC and HSC without 4th subject.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_002: ULAB gives 100% tuition scholarship to students with 5 A grades in O-Level and 2 A grades in A-Level in one sitting.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_003: ULAB gives 40% tuition scholarship to students with GPA 5.0 in both SSC and HSC including 4th subject.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_004: ULAB gives 40% tuition scholarship to siblings and spouses of current ULAB students.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_005: ULAB gives 40% tuition scholarship to ULAB and GEMCON employee children and spouses.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_006: ULAB gives 20% tuition scholarship to students with average GPA 4.50 to 4.99 in SSC and HSC.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_007: ULAB gives 15% tuition scholarship to students with average GPA 4.00 to 4.49 in SSC and HSC.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_008: ULAB gives 10% tuition scholarship to BBA students from Science background with average GPA 3.0.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_009: ULAB gives 10% tuition scholarship to female students at ULAB.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_010: ULAB gives up to 20% tuition scholarship to students from English medium school background.
ULAB_SCHOLAR_011: ULAB scholarship maximum cap is 40% except for 100% scholarship cases. Multiple scholarships cannot exceed 40%.

# RESEARCH CENTERS
ULAB_RESEARCH_001: ULAB has ten research centers that conduct research, run workshops and conferences, and promote knowledge creation.
ULAB_RESEARCH_002: ULAB Center for Sustainable Development (CSD) focuses on ethical, environmental and social sustainability research.
ULAB_RESEARCH_003: ULAB Center for Archaeological Studies (CAS) is the only research center in Bangladesh private universities dedicated to archaeological research.
ULAB_RESEARCH_004: ULAB Center for Bangla Studies (CBS) seeks to advance understanding of Bangla language, literature, and culture.
ULAB_RESEARCH_005: ULAB Center for Entrepreneurship and Sustainability (CES) is Bangladesh first university-based research center on entrepreneurship and business ethics.
